Visual style: a clean, minimalist tech product-launch keynote — the look of a
modern launch film or stage presentation (Apple keynote / Linear launch
energy). Dark, calm, premium. The interface images are the demo; everything
around them is a quiet stage.

LAYOUT & BACKGROUNDS
- Treat each frame like a keynote slide: one idea, huge negative space.
- Hero moments on a deep charcoal / near-black "stage" background; the
  product interface image centered and large, floating with a soft shadow,
  like a demo screen on stage. Lighter warm-neutral frames may alternate for
  quieter beats.
- Flat, uncluttered backgrounds only. No busy gradients, mesh blobs, stock
  photos, textures, 3D renders, or decorative clip art.

TYPOGRAPHY & STAT MOMENTS
- Clean modern sans-serif (geometric / grotesque). Very large, calm headlines.
- Keynote-style stat slides are encouraged: a single huge number or phrase
  alone on the stage — "4096px", "One undo step", "Tag once. Pour forever." —
  then back to the interface.
- Minimal on-screen text otherwise: short labels, sentence case, no bullet
  walls. The narration carries the detail.

COLOR
- Mostly neutral: charcoal, near-black, soft off-white, light grey.
- ONE restrained accent — magenta/pink (#C81E67) — for the tagline, key
  stats, thin underlines, and the highlight that points at the control being
  described. Never neon, never flooded.

MOTION & PACING
- Confident and unhurried: smooth fades, slow push-ins on the interface, a
  gentle spotlight or highlight drawing the eye to the control being
  discussed. Let stat slides hold for a beat.
- No flashy transitions, spins, bounces, sparkles, or fast cuts.

USE THE SOURCE VISUALS
- The interface images from the source ARE the demo — show the matching one
  large while each feature is presented; don't generate alternate or
  fictional UI. Style the stage around them, not the interface itself.
- The narration never mentions the source material — voice and on-screen
  text refer only to the product ("the Photos tab", "Auto-assign"), never to
  "the document", "the guide", or "the screenshot".

AVOID: emojis, stickers, meme/comic styling, glow/neon, 3D renders, busy
collages, stock imagery, and tutorial-style cursor clutter.
