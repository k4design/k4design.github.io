Visual style: a clean, minimalist technology-company product walkthrough —
calm, precise, and premium, like a modern SaaS onboarding video (Linear /
Vercel / Apple keynote energy). The screenshots are the star; everything around
them is quiet.

LAYOUT & BACKGROUNDS
- Lots of negative space. One idea per frame — never crowd two UI screenshots
  together.
- Present each plugin screenshot large and centered on a plain, soft neutral
  background (near-white warm grey, or a deep charcoal for dark frames), with a
  gentle soft shadow and generous margins, as if it's floating on a clean canvas.
- Flat, uncluttered backgrounds only. No busy gradients, mesh blobs, stock
  photos, textures, 3D, or decorative clip art.

COLOR
- Mostly neutral: soft off-white, light grey, charcoal, black text.
- ONE restrained accent — magenta/pink (#C81E67) — used sparingly for a thin
  underline, a small highlight ring, or the callout that points at the exact
  button being described. Never flood the frame with color or use neon.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Clean modern sans-serif (geometric / grotesque). Large, calm headings.
- Minimal on-screen text: short labels only, sentence case, no dense bullet
  lists or paragraphs on screen. Let the narration carry the detail.
- Optional tiny step label in a corner (e.g. "Step 3 · Tag"), understated.

MOTION & PACING
- Subtle, smooth motion: gentle fades and slides, a soft zoom or a highlight
  that draws the eye to the specific control being explained.
- Unhurried pacing — one control per beat, each screenshot given room to breathe.
- No flashy transitions, spins, bounces, sparkles, or fast cuts.

USE THE SOURCE VISUALS
- Keep the plugin's own screenshots from the document as the on-screen visuals;
  don't generate alternate or fictional UI. Style the framing around them, not
  the interface itself.

AVOID: emojis, stickers, meme/comic styling, heavy drop shadows, glow/neon, 3D
renders, busy collages, and stock imagery.
