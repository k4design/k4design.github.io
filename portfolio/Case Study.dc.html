Proof 01 / Case study

Aperture Global Brand System

ClientAperture Global Real Estate Year2025 RoleDesign lead, brand systems OutcomeOne system, forty markets, zero rebuilds
Challenge

A fast growing luxury brokerage had strong listings and no shared language. Every market office was rebuilding decks, mailers, and reports from scratch, so the brand looked different in every city it entered.

System

I built the guide, the template kit, and the production rules together. Type, grid, and photography standards on one side, and on the other a set of files an agent can open on a Monday and ship by noon. The system carries its own instructions.

Result

Launch materials now go out in hours instead of weeks, campaign reporting reads the same in every market, and the brand holds together as the network grows. The proof is that nobody asks me how to use it.
