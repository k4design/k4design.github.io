Section 05 / The Factory Manifest / Eleven products

The Factory. Tools that ship the work.

Products built to remove friction from real production pipelines. Every one of these started as a bottleneck in my own week.

dezzy Magazine Tag a luxury property template once, then pour a hundred pages of listing content in one pass. Shipped dezzy AutoFill Tag any flyer or listing sheet once, then fill every property from a form and a photo shoot. Shipped Mockup Forge Drop artwork into a frame and get it back photographically warped onto phones, mugs, shirts and billboards. Beta Mockup Mason Bake displacement, shadow and highlight maps from a real product photo, straight into a Mockup Forge item. Beta Impose Pro Print imposition tooling for production ready layouts. Shipped CSV Importer Map each CSV column onto a named layer once, then populate every card as the rows run over. Shipped TR Text Replacer + Tags Name a layer and it becomes an editable field; tags swap one phrase across dozens of layers. Beta Creative Direction A creative director's dashboard over monday.com, on a Mac window or a phone screen. Beta Frame to MP4 Export a run of frames as one video, embedded videos and audio included, encoded locally. In review MidJourney Prompt Injector Separate a batch of prompts with a $ and it types every one into the MidJourney prompt bar. Beta MidJourney Abducter Describe a subject once, then collect matching MidJourney explore images at full resolution. Beta
Section 06 / Info Orlando, FL
Kyle Foreman / Orlando, FL

I am a designer and creative operations lead in Orlando. I have spent about fifteen years moving between print floors, brand systems, and product teams, and the through line is always the same. I design the thing, then I design the way it gets made. Lately that means brand systems for luxury real estate, campaign pipelines that a two person team can actually run, and a small suite of tools I build for myself and then hand to everyone else.
