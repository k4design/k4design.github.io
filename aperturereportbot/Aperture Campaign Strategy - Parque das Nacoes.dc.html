The Approach

Where the buyers actually are.

A residence in Parque das Nações draws two very different buyers, and they do not read the same advertisement. One is already in Portugal, lives in Lisbon, and knows the district. The other is watching Portugal from London, Paris, Zurich, or New York. Rather than compromise with a single campaign that speaks imperfectly to both, your home will be advertised twice over, in Portuguese and in English, each written for its own audience and leading to a page about your home in that same language.
