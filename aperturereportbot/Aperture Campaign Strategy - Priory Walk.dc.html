The Approach

Precision over volume.

Your home is not advertised everywhere. It is advertised where it matters. A rebuilt freehold house on a Kensington side street is bought by a small number of people, and they are not all in London. Some are already here. Others are watching London from New York, Dubai, Geneva and Hong Kong. The campaign therefore runs in two parts, one at home and one abroad, so each audience is approached on its own terms rather than through a single compromise.
