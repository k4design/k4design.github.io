The Approach

Precision over volume.

Your home is not advertised everywhere. It is advertised where it matters. A riverside duplex on the Thames at Windsor, moments from Eton High Street, is bought by a particular kind of buyer. Many are already in the United Kingdom, moving out from London or up from the Home Counties. Others are abroad, drawn to this stretch of the river by Eton and by Windsor itself. The campaign therefore runs in two parts, one at home and one abroad, so each audience is approached on its own terms.

Where It Runs

The right buyers live in the right places.

Windsor draws buyers from well beyond the Thames Valley. The domestic campaign covers the United Kingdom in full, where most buyers for a home of this kind are found. The international campaign concentrates on the places that send families to Eton and Windsor year after year: the Gulf, the financial centres of Asia, Switzerland and New York.
